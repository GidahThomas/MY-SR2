    Help

    Frequently asked questions

    Quick answers for applicants, students and staff.

    How do I apply for admission?

    Fill in the online application form and choose the programme you want to study. Admissions staff review every application, and the decision is sent to the email address you give.

    How do I pay my fees?

    All university fees are government payments made through GePG. Sign in and open Finance to get a GePG control number for what you are paying.

    Pay that control number through the Government Payments option of M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money or HaloPesa, or at a CRDB, NMB or NBC bank branch. Your payment is recorded once it is confirmed through GePG.

    I forgot my password. What do I do?

    On the sign-in page, choose Forgot Password? and enter your email address. A link to set a new password is emailed to you.

    How do staff get an account?

    Staff accounts are added by a university administrator, who gives you your username and a temporary password. Contact the administration office if you need one. Only students create their own accounts, with Create Student Account on the sign-in page.

    How many courses do I register for each semester?

    Every student registers for 6 or 7 courses a semester, within the registration window shown under Key dates and in the academic calendar.

    Who do I contact for help?

    Signed-in students and staff can raise a request with the right office through the Support Centre. Our contact details are at the bottom of this page.

    Ready to begin?

    Apply for admission online, or sign in to continue where you left off.
