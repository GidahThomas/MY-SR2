Student By-Laws and Regulations

Every student is required to read, understand and comply with these regulations.

These by-laws apply to every registered student of the University and govern academic conduct, discipline, use of University facilities and the rights and responsibilities of students. Breach of any provision below may result in disciplinary action under the procedures set out in Section 6.

  • Plagiarism, collusion, fabrication of data and impersonation in examinations or coursework are strictly prohibited and constitute academic misconduct.
  • All submitted coursework, assignments and dissertations must be the student's own original work, properly referenced where external sources are used.
  • Any student found guilty of academic misconduct is subject to penalties ranging from a zero mark on the affected assessment to suspension or expulsion, depending on severity and any prior record.

  • Students must attend at least 75% of scheduled lectures, seminars and practical sessions for each registered course to be eligible to sit the end-of-semester examination.
  • A student who falls below the minimum attendance threshold without an approved excuse (medical certificate or written authorization from the Dean of Students) may be barred from the examination for that course.
  • Attendance is recorded by the course lecturer and is visible to the student via the Attendance module.

  • Students must present a valid University identity card or registration number to sit any examination.
  • Unauthorized materials, electronic devices and communication with other candidates during an examination are prohibited.
  • A student must have a clear fee balance and a completed course registration for the relevant semester to be issued an examination slip.
  • Requests for re-marking or appeal against a published result must be submitted through the Requests module within two weeks of publication.

  • Students are expected to conduct themselves with honesty, courtesy and respect towards staff, fellow students and the wider community at all times.
  • Harassment, discrimination, violence and substance abuse on University premises are strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.
  • Students must comply with the acceptable-use policy for University ICT systems (this system included) - accounts must not be shared, and any attempt to access another user's data without authorization is a serious offence.

  • Students allocated University accommodation must comply with hall rules, including quiet hours, guest policies and care of University property. Damage may result in a charge to the student's fee account.
  • Library materials must be returned or renewed by their due date. Overdue items accrue a daily fine and may result in a hold on further borrowing until resolved.

  • Alleged breaches of these by-laws are investigated by the Head of Department in the first instance, with escalation to the University Disciplinary Committee for serious matters.
  • A student has the right to be heard before any disciplinary sanction is imposed, and may appeal a decision through the Complaints module.
  • Students may raise a grievance regarding academic or administrative matters at any time via the Requests or Complaints modules; these are logged, tracked and routed to the appropriate office.
